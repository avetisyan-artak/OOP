Student - This is the base class, representing any student at a university.
It may contain common attributes (data members) such as name, ID, course, grades, etc.

UndergraduateStudent - Inherits from Student.
Represents students who are studying at the undergraduate (bachelor’s) level.

GraduateStudent - Also inherits from Student.
Represents students who are in graduate-level programs (master’s or doctoral studies).

Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior - These all inherit from UndergraduateStudent.
They represent the four years (or levels) of an undergraduate program:

Freshman - first-year student

Sophomore - second-year student

Junior - third-year student

Senior - fourth-year student

MastersStudent and DoctoralStudent - Inherit from GraduateStudent.

MastersStudent - represents a master’s degree student

DoctoralStudent - represents a PhD (doctoral) student
