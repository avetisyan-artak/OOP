1. Base class: Shape
Represents any geometric shape.
Could include general attributes such as color, position, or area/volume calculation methods (conceptually).

2. TwoDimensionalShape (2D)
Represents flat shapes - those that exist in two dimensions (length and width).
Common examples:
    Circle - defined by a radius.
    Ellipse - more general than a circle (has two radii).
    Polygon - a closed figure with straight sides.

3. Subclasses of Polygon

Triangle - 3 sides.
RightTriangle  one right angle.
EquilateralTriangle - all sides equal.
Rectangle - 4 right angles.
Square - special rectangle with all sides equal.
Pentagon, Hexagon, etc. - regular polygons with 5, 6, or more sides.

4. ThreeDimensionalShape (3D)

Represents solid shapes - those that have length, width, and height.
Common examples:
    Sphere - perfectly round 3D shape.
    Cone - has a circular base and a pointed top.
    Cylinder - has two parallel circular bases.
    Polyhedron - solid shape with flat polygonal faces.

5. Subclasses of Polyhedron

Cube - all faces are equal squares.
Pyramid - polygon base with triangular sides meeting at an apex.
Prism - two parallel, congruent polygon bases connected by rectangular faces.
