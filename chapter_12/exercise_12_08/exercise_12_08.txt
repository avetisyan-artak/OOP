Quadrilateral - Base class

The most general four-sided figure.
    All others inherit from this.

Trapezoid - Derived from Quadrilateral
    Adds the property of one pair of parallel sides.

Parallelogram - Derived from Trapezoid
    Adds the property of two pairs of parallel sides.

Rectangle - Derived from Parallelogram
    Adds the property of four right angles.

Square - Derived from Rectangle
    Adds the property of four equal sides.
